About

Advancing Sustainable Development through Urban & Transportation Planning, Geospatial Science, Housing & Real Estate, and Environmental Research

I am MD. Toufiq Hossain, an Urban Planner and Researcher holding a Bachelor’s degree from Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (RUET), where I ranked 9th in my class (out of 56) with a CGPA of 3.58/4.00 and received a merit-based technical scholarship in the 2nd, 3rd and 4th academic years from the URP Department.

I am a Certified Professional Urban Planner (BIP Membership ID: 2377) and currently work as Assistant Town Planner at Eastern Housing Limited, designing residential site layouts and managing spatial databases. Previously, as Assistant Urban Planner at Sheltech Consultants (Pvt.) Ltd., I contributed to Upazila and Pourashava Master Plans, transportation planning, GIS-based spatial analysis, environmental mapping and community engagement research.

My research spans land use dynamics, biodiversity, transportation, disaster risk assessment, community-based tourism and economic regionalization, with Q1 journal articles in Environmental Sciences Europe (IF 7.5) and the International Journal of Community Well-Being (IF 3.5), a Springer book chapter, a Journal of Bangladesh Institute of Planners article, and multiple conference papers.
